# Three-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00 - Hook

Most Slack agents tell you what happened. SignalRoom tells you what will break next.

Optional reset before recording:

```text
/signalroom clean
```

## 0:20 - Problem

Launch risks hide in scattered Slack threads, tickets, docs, and calendar events. Nobody sees the full picture until the deadline slips.

## 0:40 - Ask The Agent

Run:

```text
/signalroom launch
```

Narration: SignalRoom searches live context, maps decisions and blockers, and produces a launch risk readout.

## 1:10 - Show The Wow Moment

Point out:

- high launch risk
- payment webhook blocker
- QA and launch-date contradiction
- missing pricing owner
- overloaded critical engineer
- evidence citations

## 1:50 - What-If Simulation

Run:

```text
/signalroom whatif payment slips 2 days
```

Narration: SignalRoom does not just summarize. It predicts downstream impact and recommends a rescue path.

## 2:20 - Decision Timeline

Run:

```text
/signalroom timeline
```

Narration: The agent reconstructs how the team got here, with citations.

## 2:40 - Rescue Brief

Run:

```text
/signalroom brief
```

Narration: One click turns scattered context into an executive-ready plan.

## 3:00 - Close

SignalRoom makes Slack proactive. It helps teams catch risks while there is still time to fix them.
